## What the brochure highlights

**Generous proportions.** 4743/1900/1720 mm with a 2720 mm wheelbase — the
brochure says this gives room for seven passengers plus luggage space, while
keeping it easy to drive, park and manoeuvre. 20-inch alloy rims.

**Commanding exterior.** Chrome accents across the front profile and distinctive
LED lighting. At the back, dual exhaust tips and a rear diffuser, with a smart
LED tail lamp carrying integrated turn indicators and retro reflectors.

**Panoramic view.** A full panoramic roof that fills the cabin with natural
light — the brochure's word is "openness".

**Powertrain.** A 1.5-litre turbocharged engine delivering 156 hp with a
6-speed dual-clutch transmission. Dual drive modes (Economy / Sport) adapt to
different conditions.

**Chic and classy.** Premium interior materials, 360-degree soft-touch surfaces
and chrome accents.

**Technology.** An all-digital 12.3-inch instrument cluster, a 10.2-inch touch
infotainment display with Apple CarPlay and Android, and **8 speakers spread
through the cabin** for what the brochure calls "an exceptional sound system".

**The perfect family companion.** A roomy 5/7 seater configuration, with premium
cabin space.

**التقنية.** شاشة عدادات رقمية 12.3 بوصة، وشاشة معلومات وترفيه 10.2 بوصة تدعم
Apple CarPlay وAndroid، و**8 مكبرات صوت** موزّعة في المقصورة.
