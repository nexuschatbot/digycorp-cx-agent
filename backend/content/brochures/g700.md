# Jetour G700

The flagship. A plug-in hybrid, body-on-frame SUV — the only PHEV in the range
alongside the T1 and T2 i-DM, the only one with genuine off-road hardware, and by
some distance the most powerful and most expensive car Elite sells.

Three variants: **Comfort, Luxury, Flagship**. A fourth, **G700 Top Fire**, is
coming soon and has no specification, price or stock.

## Two things worth knowing before recommending it

**It answers a commuting question better than anything else in the range.** WLTC
pure electric range is **80–100 km**. A customer describing a daily commute
shorter than that would do most of their driving on electricity — the combined
WLTC figure of **1.39 L/100km** reflects exactly that, and is not a number to
quote on its own. The honest petrol-only figure is the **feeder consumption,
8.8 L/100km** (measured 8.6). Elite's own catalogue figure is **10–11.6 km/L**.

**It has the most speakers in the range by a wide margin** — 18 on Luxury and
Flagship, 12 on Comfort, against 8 on the X70 FL Plus and 6 on the X50. If a
customer asks for the best sound system by count, this is the answer. The X70 FL
Plus's system is branded (SONY); the G700's is not named in the brochure. Both
facts are true and neither settles it — offer both and let them choose.
