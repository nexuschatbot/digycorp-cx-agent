**A note on seats, because the two sources disagree in tone.** *Both* brochures
market a "roomy 5/7 seater configuration" in their marketing pages. The
specification tables are where they differ: the X70 FL's says `5/5+2`, the X70 FL
Plus's says `7`, with a removable second row and a foldable, removable third row.
Quote the specification table, not the marketing line.

## What the brochure highlights

**The pitch.** "The perfect companion for enjoying a leisurely afternoon around
the city." A 1.6L TGDI engine and an agile suspension system.

**Generous proportions.** 4724/1900/1720 mm on a 2720 mm wheelbase — room for
seven people in comfort plus space for their luggage, while keeping driving,
parking and manoeuvring smooth and easy.

**Elegant exterior.** Chromed accents underlining the front, distinctive LED
illuminations. 20-inch lightweight alloy rims, which the brochure credits with
adding stability *and* fuel efficiency.

**Backdrop.** An uninterrupted LED stripe runs the full width of the rear. Quad
exhaust tips integrated into the bumper.

**Panoramic view.** A full panoramic roof.

**Spacious, convenient interior.** A roomy 5/7 seater configuration. Front seat
ventilation, heated and cooled. Refrigeration *and* heating of the armrest box.
Folding rear seats for extra storage, with an automatic boot.

**Chic and classy.** Leather and high-quality fabric. 6-direction electrical
seats with lumbar support. A wireless charging pad. Ambient lighting in **7
colour combinations**. **8 speakers spread through the cabin for an exceptional
surround sound system.**

**Technology.** An all-digital instrument cluster. A touchscreen panel with an
intelligent air-conditioning zoning system. A **10.25-inch** touch multimedia
interface. Bluetooth, screen mirroring, one rear USB port and one Type-C port for
charging. A 3-spoke multi-function steering wheel in perforated leather.

**Intelligent safety.** LDWS (lane departure warning) with blind spot monitoring,
AEBS (advanced emergency braking) with forward collision warning, and adaptive
cruise control.
