# Jetour T2 Luxury Plus

A boxy, four-wheel-drive petrol SUV. **Five seats** — despite the size — with an
8-speed automatic, 220 mm of ground clearance and a SONY twelve-speaker system.

The other T2 in the range, the **T2 i-DM**, is a separate model with a plug-in
hybrid powertrain — not a variant of this one.

## Where it sits in the range

**It is four-wheel drive.** Only this and the G700 are. Every other petrol model
is front-wheel drive.

**It is five seats, not seven.** Bigger on the outside than an X70 FL Plus, and
seats two fewer people. A customer who wants space *and* seven seats needs the
X70 FL Plus; a customer who wants space and capability wants this.

**Its audio beats the X70s.** SONY with **12 speakers**, against 8 in the X70 FL
Plus and 6 in the X50. Only the G700 Luxury and Flagship have more (18).
