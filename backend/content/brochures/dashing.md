## Not in this brochure

**Say "I don't have that" rather than estimating any of the following. This file
is deliberately incomplete and marked `draft-partial`.**

- **Engine power for the 1.5 Standard**, and maximum power for the 1.6 Luxury.
  Those rows shred under extraction and the marketing pages have not been read.
  **Do not infer one variant's output from the other's** — they are different
  engines (1.5TCI vs 1.6TGDI) with different gearboxes.
- The 1.5's spec page shows **both `1,498` and `1598`** in its displacement row.
  1498 is the 1.5's; 1598 belongs to the 1.6. Column bleed, not a variant.
- Body and interior colours.
- Luggage capacity, kerb weight, maximum speed, 0–100 km/h, towing.
- Speaker count for the **1.5 Standard** — only the 1.6 Luxury's 8 could be read.
- Marketing pages for both PDFs.
- Pricing — catalogue only (89,000 / 114,000).
