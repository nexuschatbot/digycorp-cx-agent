# Jetour T1

A boxy, body-on-frame-styled petrol SUV with a 2.0 turbo and an 8-speed
automatic. **Five seats** (2+3) in all three variants.

Three variants: **Comfort, Luxury, Flagship** — and the ordering is not what the
names suggest. See below.

## ⚠️ The trim ladder is not Comfort → Luxury → Flagship

**Luxury is the four-wheel-drive, off-road variant and the best equipped of the
three. Flagship is two-wheel drive and specified close to Comfort.**

| | Comfort | **Luxury** | Flagship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drive | 2WD (FF) | **4WD** — real-time BorgWarner | 2WD (FF) |
| Speakers | 8 | **9, SONY** | 8 |
| Display screen | 12.8" | **15.6"** | 12.8" |
| Main seat function | Ventilation | **Memory + ventilation** | Ventilation |
| Central differential | — | **Limited-slip** | — |
| Rear differential | — | **Rear electronic LSD** | — |
| Off-road drive modes | Sports / Economy / Standard | **+ Snow / Mud / Sand / Rock** | Sports / Economy / Standard |
| Wading radar | — | **Yes** | — |
| Kerb weight | 1726 kg | 1833 kg | 1726 kg |
| 0–100 km/h | 8.6 s | 8.8 s | 8.6 s |

This is why the workbook prices **Luxury (129,000) above Flagship (125,785)** —
that ordering is correct, not a transposition. Do not "fix" it.
