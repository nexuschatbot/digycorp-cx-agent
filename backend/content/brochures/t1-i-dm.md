# Jetour T1 i-DM

The plug-in hybrid T1 — same body as the petrol T1, entirely different
powertrain. A 1.5-litre engine and a single P3 motor combining for **335 hp and
530 Nm**, with **up to 1,000 km of combined range**.

Second most economical car in the range on Elite's figures: **15.8–18.5 km/L**,
just behind the T2 i-DM.

## Where it sits in the range

**Same body as the petrol T1**, to the millimetre — 4705 × 1967 × 1843 on a
2800 mm wheelbase. A customer comparing them is comparing powertrains, not size.

**Far more powerful than the petrol T1 on combined output** — 335 hp against
254 bhp — while using roughly half the fuel.

**The brochure claims up to 90% pure electric driving.** That is the brochure's
own phrasing and belongs to it, not to us; quote it as their claim.

## Not in this brochure

**Say "I don't have that" rather than estimating any of the following. This file
is marked `draft-partial`.**

- **Seat count could not be established.** The petrol T1 is 5 (2+3) and this
  shares its body, but the spec pages never state it and **the body being
  identical does not prove the seating is**. Ask, or check with Elite.
- **Marketing pages 1–15 and 20 have not been read.** The "up to 90% pure
  electric driving" line was picked up incidentally; there is likely more.
- **Pure electric range on its own.** The brochure gives *combined* range (up to
  1,000 km) but no EV-only figure — unlike the G700, which publishes 80–100 km.
  Do not infer one from battery size.
- Charging times, fast or slow.
- Kerb weight, luggage capacity, ground clearance, tank volume, maximum speed,
  0–100 km/h, towing.
- Whether the two configuration columns correspond to purchasable trims.
- Pricing — catalogue only (144,000).
