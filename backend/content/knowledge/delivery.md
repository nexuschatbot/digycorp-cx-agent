# Delivery times

**21 days**, from payment and document approval — **for a car already in stock**.

If the colour or trim the customer wants is not on the ground, it is a factory
order and Elite adds **4 to 8 weeks** on top.

| | |
|---|---|
| In stock | **21 days** after payment and document approval |
| Not in stock | **21 days + 4–8 weeks** for the factory order |
| G700 Top Fire | **Coming soon — not yet available for order** |

This is the same for every model. Elite states no difference between the G700
and the X50.

## Check stock before quoting a date

The 21-day figure is only true of a car we have. **Availability is live data, not
something to assume** — the stock service answers it per variant, colour and
branch, and the honest sequence is:

1. Confirm the variant and colour the customer wants.
2. Check availability at their branch, or anywhere.
3. Quote **21 days** if it is there; quote **21 days plus 4–8 weeks** if it is a
   factory order, and say which of the two you are quoting.

Never give the 21-day number without knowing which case applies. A customer told
"three weeks" who then waits three months did not mishear us.

## Not in this file

**Say "I don't have that" rather than estimating any of the following.**

- **What "document approval" involves**, or how long it takes. The 21 days start
  after it, so a customer asking "from today?" cannot be answered from here —
  see `registration.md` for what the showroom collects.
- **Whether the 21 days are calendar or business days.** Elite wrote "21 Days".
- **Whether delivery is to the showroom or to an address.**
- **Where in the 4–8 week band a particular factory order falls**, or what moves
  it. Quote the band, never a point inside it.
- **Anything that makes a factory order faster**, including whether one can be
  expedited at all.
- **A delivery date for the G700 Top Fire.** It is not orderable, so it has no
  delivery time — not even a long one.
