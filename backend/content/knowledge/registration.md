# Registration timeline

The showroom's PRO handles registration on the customer's behalf. Digital
Mulkiya is issued the same day and **is valid to drive on immediately**.

| # | Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Document submission — Emirates ID + UAE driving licence | Same day as booking confirmation |
| 2 | Insurance certificate issued | Same day, once financing or payment is confirmed |
| 3 | RTA registration processing — **digital Mulkiya** | **10–30 minutes** |
| 4 | Physical registration card and plates — standard | Same-day pickup, or 1–2 business days by courier |
| 5 | Physical registration card and plates — express | **2 hours** (extra fee) |
| 6 | Roadworthiness inspection | **Not required** |
| 7 | Personalised / premium plate | Varies — **hand off to a person** |

## The three things worth saying unprompted

**Registration cannot start until insurance is issued.** That is the real
dependency in this list, and it is why stage 2 sits where it does. A customer
chasing their plates is usually waiting on insurance.

**The digital Mulkiya is enough to drive.** 10–30 minutes at the RTA, and they
can drive away — the physical card and plates follow. Customers often assume
they are stuck until the plastic arrives.

**No roadworthiness inspection for three years.** New vehicles are exempt from
the RTA technical inspection. Volunteer this; it is good news and people expect
the opposite.

## What the customer needs to bring

Emirates ID and a UAE driving licence. The showroom PRO collects both and
submits them.

## Express plates

Two hours instead of same-day-or-next-day, for **an additional express-delivery
fee**. Elite does not state the amount — say the fee exists and that the
showroom will confirm it, never a number.

## Personalised or premium plates

A separate RTA selection or auction process, **outside this flow entirely**.
Hand off to a person rather than estimating a timeline or a price.

## Not in this file

**Say "I don't have that" rather than estimating any of the following.**

- **The express-delivery fee**, or the standard registration fee beyond the
  AED 1,000 registration component in the on-road price.
- **Which RTA centre**, or whether the customer must attend in person.
- **What happens for a non-resident, a company purchase, or a transfer.**
- **How long insurance issuance takes** when financing is still being approved —
  stage 2 says "once confirmed", and the confirmation is the bank's timeline,
  not ours.
- **Anything about personalised plates**, including cost, availability or how the
  auction works. Hand off.
- **Whether any of this differs by emirate.** The sheet says RTA throughout,
  which is Dubai's authority; Abu Dhabi, Ajman and Al Ain are not addressed.
  **Worth asking Elite** — three of the five branches are outside Dubai.
