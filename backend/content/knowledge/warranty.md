# Warranty and service coverage

**Never say "the warranty is ten years" without saying which warranty.** On the
petrol cars that is the whole vehicle. On the three plug-in hybrids it is the
engine only, and the vehicle cover is shorter.

## The plug-in hybrids — four separate covers

| | Jetour G700 | Jetour T2 i-DM | Jetour T1 i-DM |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engine** | 10 years / 1,000,000 km | 10 years / 1,000,000 km | 10 years / 1,000,000 km |
| **Vehicle** | **8 years / 300,000 km** | **6 years / unlimited km** | **8 years / 300,000 km** |
| **Battery** | 8 years / 160,000 km | 8 years / 160,000 km | 8 years / 160,000 km |
| Service contract | 3 years / 30,000 km | 3 years / 30,000 km | 3 years / 30,000 km |

Note the T2 i-DM's vehicle cover is **unlimited kilometres over six years**,
where the G700's and T1 i-DM's is **300,000 km over eight**. Which is better
depends entirely on how far the customer drives — roughly 37,500 km a year is
the crossover. State both; do not rank them.

## The petrol cars — one cover

Elite states a single warranty for each of these, with no separate engine,
vehicle or battery figure.

| Model | Warranty | Service contract |
|---|---|---|
| Jetour T2 Luxury Plus | 10 years / 1,000,000 km | 3 years / 30,000 km |
| Jetour T1 | see below — this one is split | 3 years / 30,000 km |
| Jetour Dashing 1.5 Standard | 10 years / 1,000,000 km | 3 years / 30,000 km |
| Jetour Dashing 1.6 Luxury | 10 years / 1,000,000 km | 3 years / 30,000 km |
| Jetour X70 FL | 10 years / 1,000,000 km | 3 years / 30,000 km |
| Jetour X70 FL Plus | 10 years / 1,000,000 km | 3 years / 30,000 km |
| Jetour X50 | 10 years / 1,000,000 km | 3 years / 30,000 km |

**The petrol T1 is the exception among the petrol cars** and is recorded like a
hybrid, minus the battery:

| Jetour T1 | |
|---|---|
| **Engine** | 10 years / 1,000,000 km |
| **Vehicle** | **6 years / unlimited km** |
| Service contract | 3 years / 30,000 km |

## G700 Top Fire

**Coming soon.** Elite publishes no warranty for it. Say so; do not assume it
inherits the G700's.

## Not in this file

**Say "I don't have that" rather than estimating any of the following.**

- **What the warranty actually covers** — no list of included or excluded parts,
  no wear-item policy, no paint or corrosion terms. Elite supplied durations
  only.
- **Whether it transfers to a second owner.**
- **Service interval spacing** — the contract is "3 years / 30,000 km", but
  whether that is servicing every 10,000 km or on some other schedule is not
  stated.
- **What the service contract includes** — parts, labour, consumables, none of
  it is specified.
- **Cost of extending any cover.** The "Extended Peace of Mind" promotion adds
  two years of free scheduled maintenance; see `promotions.md`, and note it is
  marked Sample.
- **Whether warranty starts at purchase or at registration.**
- **Anything for the G700 Top Fire.**
